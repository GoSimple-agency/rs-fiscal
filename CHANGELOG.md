## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-15

### Added
- `Pib::isValid()` and `Pib::checkDigit()` — Serbian PIB validation (ISO 7064 MOD 11,10).
- `IpsQr::build()`, `IpsQr::normalizeAccount()` and `IpsQr::amount()` — NBS IPS QR payload
  generation.
